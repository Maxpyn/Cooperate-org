WHO WE ARE

A platform for people, ideas and opportunity.

Cashville ZMC International Business Club & Multipurpose Co-operative Society is a people-oriented organisation where individuals, entrepreneurs, professionals, farmers and communities come together.

We believe that people achieve more when they work together. Through our Business Club and co-operative initiatives, we encourage savings, entrepreneurship, skills development, agriculture, networking and community empowerment.

Our goal is to help members discover opportunities, develop their potential and participate meaningfully in activities that improve their economic, financial and social wellbeing.

We are committed to trust, accountability, collaboration, inclusion and shared growth. Ideas can become opportunities, opportunities can create value, and value can build better futures.

OUR PHILOSOPHY Empowering People • Creating Values • Building Dreams.
